Khet Sathi for AI agents

This page is for browser agents such as Meta's Muse acting for a person. Khet Sathi is a farm app for smallholder farmers in Bangladesh, India and anywhere else: fields seen by satellite, poultry sheds checked from phone videos, home yards, a farm helper called Sathi, and selling the harvest. A plain-text copy is at /llms.txt.

Rules

  1. Sign-in needs the person. Sign in sends a 6-digit code to their email. Ask the person for the code; never guess one, never try codes.
  2. Confirm before acting. Ask the person before any Book, Sell, Confirm, Save, Remove or Share step.
  3. Sathi never names a disease or a medicine, for crops or for birds. Do not ask her to. For a sick crop or bird she finds the nearest agriculture office or vet.
  4. Labels you must keep. "sample prices", "demo" and "simulated" mark things that are not real. Do not present them as real quotes, orders or calls.
  5. Calls to buyers and services in the app are demo calls and never reach a real person. Real shops and offices are only listed (Google Maps), with their phone number for the person to call.

How to find things on the page

  • Every button has a fixed data-action attribute that does not change with the language (for example data-action="addBtn"). Visible text changes with the language buttons বাংলা (Bangla), हिंदी (Hindi) and English; press English first if that helps.
  • Popups are dialogs with a name (their title). When one opens or closes, a status message says "Opened: …" or "Closed: …". Escape or the button "Close: …" closes it.
  • While the app works it shows a status message ("Measuring…", "Looking for shops near this field…"). Wait until it is gone before the next step.
  • Short notices ("Saved", errors) appear as a status message at the bottom.

Common tasks

Sign in

  1. Press Sign in. Type the person's email in the email box, press Send me a code.
  2. Ask the person for the 6-digit code from that email and type it in the code box.

Look at the demo farm (no farm of your own needed)

  1. After sign-in, open the account button and press Watch the demo farm. A bar at the top says it is the demo farm; press My farm to go back.
  2. The demo farm cannot be changed by visitors; anything saved there is refused with a notice.

Run a field camera demo story (admin, in the demo farm)

  1. Open the demo field. On Home, the Field camera card has Demo stories: Play: patch spreading (fcStory_patch), Play: field ripening (fcStory_ripe) and Play: goats walk in (fcStory_goats).
  2. Press one and wait while SAM 3.1 measures each frame. The frames are AI-generated and marked as demo. A new story replaces the one before.
  3. Then read Sathi on watch on the same page: what she saw, what she thought and what she asks the person. Do not answer her question for the person.

Ask Sathi

  1. Open Sathi. Type in the box named Ask Sathi and press Enter.
  2. Her answer appears in the conversation (a log that announces new messages). Wait until the working steps end.

Report a crop problem (measured, never diagnosed)

  1. On Home, press Take a photo of the problem (or, in the demo farm, Try with the demo leaf).
  2. Give a close photo of one leaf. Wait for the measurement (SAM 3.1 outlines the leaf and its spots), then press Next.
  3. Pick the plant (the field's crop, or Another plant and type its name), at least one thing seen, since when and how many plants.
  4. Press Make the summary. Then, with the person's OK: Send on WhatsApp (with photo), Copy, or Nearest agriculture office for the office's phone number and directions.

Add a home yard

  1. Press Add a field, shed or yard; the list of places opens. Press Add a yard (addYardBtn). Give one photo of the open space in front of the house and the location.
  2. Give the size in the local unit and how much sun it gets. Sathi says what to plant now, with a shopping list (sample prices) and real nurseries nearby.

Add a field

  1. Press Add a field, shed or yard, then Add field (addFromList). Give a photo taken in the field or a location, press Find my field.
  2. Sathi finds the field on the satellite image and checks what covers it. Pick what is growing or what to plant.

Add a poultry shed

  1. Press Add a field, shed or yard, then Add shed (addShedBtn). Give a name and about how many chickens. The sensor kit is a demo; phone videos of the shed are checked for real.

The weekly report

  1. On Home press Weekly report. It covers every field, shed and yard: the last 7 days and the week ahead. Keep it as a PDF or share it with the person's OK.

Sell the harvest

  1. Open Market. Offers come from demo calls to buyers; they are marked demo.
  2. Choose an offer and press Agree and sell only after the person agrees. Nothing is sold for real in the demo.

Where the data comes from

OpenStreetMap, ESA WorldCover, Copernicus Sentinel-2, MIRCA-OS, FAOSTAT, SoilGrids, Open-Meteo and Google Maps (shops and offices), read live. Meta's models: Muse Spark (Sathi), SAM 3.1 (outlines), Muse Voice Transcribe (voice). When a source does not answer, the app shows the last good data with the time it is from.
